Engineering Case Study: 02

An offline-first POS that never stops billing

ClientK2 Chicken, franchise retail
TimelineDec 2025, Present
RoleSoftware Development Intern, AzeelaAI Labs
StackReact · Next.js · Node.js · IndexedDB
StatusIn production, 3 locations
The Problem

When the internet goes down, everything stops.

The shops often lose internet for 4 to 6 hours. With a normal POS system, that means:

  • No way to process payments, customers leave
  • Inventory records go out of sync
  • Sales data vanishes until the connection returns

The counter must keep working with or without internet.

The Solution

Treat offline as the default state, not the failure state.

Every sale saves first to IndexedDB in the browser. When internet returns, the system syncs on its own. Staff do not need to do anything.

It also covers stock, billing, customers, loyalty, reports, and user roles for all three shops.

Product

The dashboard takes real orders, adds items to a cart, and syncs when internet is back.

Challenges
Challenge 01

How do you prevent data conflicts when the connection returns?

Three shops may sync hours of sales at once. Each sale keeps its time. The system uses that time to sort records and avoid clashes.

Challenge 02

How do you keep inventory honest across three stores?

Stock was counted by hand, so it often went wrong. Now stock updates from each sale. Counts stay correct.

Challenge 03

How much system do you give a non-technical cashier?

Staff see only billing and customers. Owners see stock, loyalty, and sales reports. Each person sees what they need.

Lessons Learned
Reliability matters more than new features. The hard part was sync and edge cases, not the screens. The part of the system users never see is the part they depend on most
