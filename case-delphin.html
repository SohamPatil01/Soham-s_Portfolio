A consulting site that sells clarity

The Problem

Consulting services are broad. The website has to make them concrete.

Delphin needed a professional web presence that covered technology, finance, operations, marketing, and automation solutions — without turning into a wall of jargon. Prospects had to find the right service quickly and trust that the firm could deliver.

The Solution

Structured sections, client-focused navigation.

I built a responsive business website with clear sections for services, case studies, selected work, and business insights. Interactive UI components and Bootstrap 5 layouts keep the experience polished on desktop and mobile while staying fast and maintainable.

Navigation is organized around what a client needs — not around how the firm is structured internally.

Challenges & Trade-offs
Challenge 01

How do you present five service lines without overwhelming visitors?

Each practice area got a dedicated section with a short value statement and a clear path to examples — so visitors scan for relevance instead of reading a brochure cover to cover.

Challenge 02

When is a framework worth it for a marketing site?

Bootstrap 5 covered responsive grid and interactive components without a heavy SPA build. That kept delivery fast while leaving room for custom CSS and JavaScript where the design needed personality.
