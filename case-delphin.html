Engineering Case Study: 04

A consulting site that sells clarity

ClientDelphin Business Consulting
TimelineJul 2026, Sep 2026
RoleFrontend developer
StackHTML5 · CSS3 · JavaScript · Bootstrap 5
StatusLive site
The Problem

Consulting services are broad. The website has to make them concrete.

Delphin needed a website for its services: technology, finance, operations, marketing, and automation. Visitors had to find the right service fast and trust the firm.

The Solution

Structured sections, client-focused navigation.

I built a responsive site with sections for services, case studies, work, and insights. Bootstrap 5 keeps the layout clean on phone and desktop.

The menu follows what a client looks for, not how the firm is set up.

Challenges
Challenge 01

How do you present five service lines without overwhelming visitors?

Each service has its own section. It has a short summary and a link to examples. Visitors can scan fast.

Challenge 02

When is a framework worth it for a marketing site?

Bootstrap 5 gave me the grid and common parts. The site stayed light. I added custom CSS and JavaScript only where needed.

Lessons Learned
A good site answers three questions fast: what you do, who it helps, and what to do next. Clarity beats completeness on first visit
