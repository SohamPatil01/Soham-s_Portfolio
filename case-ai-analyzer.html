Engineering Case Study: 03

AI that tells a business what technology it needs

TypeInternal AzeelaAI tool
TimelineDec 2025, Present
RoleSoftware Development Intern, AzeelaAI Labs
StackNext.js · Node.js · PostgreSQL · OpenAI · Gemini
StatusActive development
The Problem

Small businesses know something is broken. They don't know what to buy.

Advice to small businesses is often vague. Owners need to know where manual work costs money and what tool can help. Finding that takes time. It means checking the website, reading reviews, and comparing similar businesses.

The Solution

Automate the research. Deliver a verdict, not a data dump.

The tool reads the business website, Google profile, and reviews. OpenAI and Gemini study this data. The result is a clear report with actions, such as a POS system, booking tool, or stock system. Each action links to evidence.

Reports open in a secure dashboard. They can become sales leads with a clear next step.

Challenges
Challenge 01

How do you get consistent, structured output from LLMs?

Model replies can be messy. I made the model reply in a fixed format. The app checks the format before it shows on screen.

Challenge 02

Why orchestrate two model providers instead of one?

Each provider can fail in a different way. Using two providers keeps the tool working if one is down or slow.

Challenge 03

How does analysis become revenue?

Each report can become a lead with a clear next step. The team can track it and act on it.

Lessons Learned
The hard part is not calling the model. It is clear output, checks, and a workflow people can use. AI is a component, the product is the system
