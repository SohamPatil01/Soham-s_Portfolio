Engineering Case Study: 01

From WhatsApp chaos to a two-minute order

ClientK2 Chicken, commercial ordering
TimelineDec 2025, Present
RoleSoftware Development Intern, AzeelaAI Labs
StackNext.js · React · Node.js
StatusLive, in commercial use
The Problem

Every order is a back-and-forth. Every conversation takes time.

Orders arrived as WhatsApp messages. Addresses were often incomplete and items were unclear. Each order took about 8 minutes and errors were common.

The team could only take as many orders as they could type.

The Solution

Structure at the source: the customer does the data entry.

I built an online store with a product list, cart, and a checked checkout form. Orders arrive complete, on phone or desktop.

Order time fell from 8 minutes to under 2 minutes. Nothing is retyped, so fewer errors occur.

Challenges
Challenge 01

How do you migrate customers who are used to WhatsApp?

The site had to be easier than sending a WhatsApp message. So I kept the steps few and made it work well on phones.

Challenge 02

Where does order accuracy actually come from?

Accuracy comes from the form, not from care. Fields are checked as the customer types. This removes most errors before the order is sent.
